One line, at most 200 characters, roasting a smart fridge — its app, its firmware, its ice maker — for make.fun's "Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh" bounty. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, where the house judge reads it.

The line: *"My fridge says my milk is at 12%. Twelve percent of what — hope? It's milk, not a phone battery. Stop giving my groceries a health bar."*

Why this line: it roasts the fridge's app for treating groceries like a phone battery, it is specific to smart fridges (no other appliance reports a freshness percentage), every word earns its place in 135 characters, and it dodges the obvious cracks about ordering groceries or watching you.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
